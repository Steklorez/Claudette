# Away from your desk

Claude can work for a long time, and you don't have to sit and watch. This chapter covers how Claudette tells you when something needs you, how the Dock or taskbar icon shows what your tabs are doing, how to reach a tab from your phone, how to stop the computer sleeping in the middle of a task, and how to have Claudette start when you log in.
