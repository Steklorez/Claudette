# Claudette

Claudette is the app's pixel mascot. She stands on the message box, keeps you company while you work, and reacts to what your tabs are doing. This chapter covers what she does and how to change or hide her, and the small games you can play against her while Claude works.

Everything about her is optional, and none of it costs usage or touches Claude Code.
