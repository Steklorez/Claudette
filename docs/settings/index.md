# Settings

Claudette's settings are in one window, with a page for each part of the app. This chapter covers the window itself, a table of every setting with its default and where it's explained, the two looks Claudette has and what it does for people who need less motion or a screen reader, how to change its shortcuts, and the Advanced page.

Each setting is explained on the page about the part it changes. [Every setting](every-setting.md) is the way in from the other direction: find the setting, then follow its link.
