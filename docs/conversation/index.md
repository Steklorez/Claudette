# The conversation

Each tab shows its conversation as a column of cards: what you sent, what Claude replied, the tools it used, and how each turn ended. This chapter covers reading it, finding your way around a long one, copying from it, taking notes beside it, exporting it, going back to an earlier message, and the images and files in it.

Answering Claude's questions and permission prompts is in [Permissions and prompts](../permissions/index.md). Writing your own messages is in [The composer](../composer/index.md).
