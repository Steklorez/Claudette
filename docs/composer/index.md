# The composer

The composer is the message box at the foot of every tab, with the bar of choices and counts under it and the status line under that. This chapter covers sending a message and stopping Claude, the bar, the `/` and `@` lists, attachments and large pastes, what Claudette keeps of what you type, quick suffixes, choosing the model and effort, prompt suggestions and check-ins on long turns.

Claudette herself stands on the box's top edge. She has [her own chapter](../claudette/index.md).
